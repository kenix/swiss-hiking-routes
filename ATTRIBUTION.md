# Attribution

The route pack is derived from **Wanderland**, the SchweizMobil hiking routes
published as open government data by the Federal Roads Office FEDRO (ASTRA):

- Dataset: `ch.astra.wanderland`, https://data.geo.admin.ch/ch.astra.wanderland/
- Licence: **Opendata BY** (free use, including commercial, with the source named),
  https://www.geo.admin.ch/en/general-terms-of-use-fsdi
- Source to name: "Source: ASTRA / SchweizMobil" (German: "Quelle: ASTRA / SchweizMobil")

The names, numbers, lengths, heights, times and grades are ASTRA's as published;
the lines are simplified to about 20 m. SchweizMobil's own route pages at
https://schweizmobil.ch stay the authority on current closures and detours.
